How AI Detection Works

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)

  • Layer 1: Detects edges, colors
  • Layer 2: Recognizes shapes
  • Layer 3: Identifies combinations (wings, propellers)
  • Output: "This is a drone!"

YOLOv8 Magic: Single-pass detection is 50x faster than traditional methods
